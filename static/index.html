Things that catch people out

  • Case. Upper and lower case are the same unless Match case is on, as in plain search.
  • Special characters. { } ( ) [ ] . * + ? | ^ $ \ mean something. Put \ before one to match it: \{, \., \(.
  • Spaces are exact. a b needs exactly one space. Use \s* (any, even none) or \s+ (at least one).
  • One line at a time. . and \s stop at the end of a line. Use \n or (?s) to match across lines.
  • Literal text? Turn Regex off: plain search matches what you type, quotes included.
